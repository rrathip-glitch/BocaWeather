How this forecast works

Two models, one verdict. We pull HRRR (NOAA's high-res convective model — sharp on Florida storms) and AIFS (ECMWF's AI global model). We compare them hour-by-hour.

When they agree, trust it. When they disagree, the chart shows an amber band and the verdict softens to CAUTION. Disagreement is itself a forecast — your edge as a player is knowing the call is uncertain.

Verdict thresholds: GO when max rain probability stays under 35%. CAUTION between 35–60% or when models disagree. NO-GO above 60% or with appreciable accumulation expected.

~60 minutes before play, also pull up RainViewer or RadarScope to scan for individual convective cells — radar trumps models inside the 1-hour window.
